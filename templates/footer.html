What is SAXAZ?

SAXAZ is an online study portal that provides structured lessons, explanations, and practice content based on your class and syllabus.

Content is released in parts called issues. This is an extension of the content in Labour India Magazine.

Where do I find my study content and exams?

Open Exams from your Dashboard to browse your subjects, chapters, lessons, and practice exams, or open Digi Library for digital books and question banks.

How do I update my details?

Go to the Profile section in the sidebar to update your information.
