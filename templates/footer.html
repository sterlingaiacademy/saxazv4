What is on my Dashboard?

Open Dashboard from the left navigation menu to see your class curriculum overview, active Labour India study issues, daily progress, and quick resume shortcuts.

Where do I find Subjects & Exams and Quiz Points?

Select Subjects & Exams to explore subjects, chapter lessons, videos, tutor explanations, Mock Exams, and proctored Real Exams. Check Quiz Points to review your completed exam attempts and scores.

How does Rewards & Shop work?

You earn Coins as you complete lessons, quizzes, and study milestones. Open Rewards & Shop (or click the Coins badge in the header) to redeem Coins for vouchers and study rewards.

How do I adjust Profile & Voice settings?

Open Profile & Voice in the sidebar to choose your preferred teacher voice, mute or unmute spoken explanations, adjust reading font size, or update your account details.

How do Parent Portal & PIN work?

Parents sign in with the student's Login ID and password using the Parent Portal tab on the Login page, or switch directly via Parent Portal in the sidebar. Inside Settings & PIN, parents can set a 4-digit security PIN to lock Parent Portal and switch back anytime via Student View.

Where can I buy Labour India magazines?

You can subscribe to or purchase Labour India print issues online at www.labourindia.com.
