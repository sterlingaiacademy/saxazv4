CHAPTER IN A NUT SHELL

Hey {{ first_name }}! Do you know why we are alive? Inside our body, four very important jobs are happening all the time, even when we are sleeping, sitting, or playing. We call them Life Processes. If even one of them stops, we cannot survive. Let us understand each one in detail.

1. Nutrition - Food For Energy And Growth

Nutrition means taking in food and using it for energy, growth, and repair of the body. There are two types. In plants, it is called Autotrophic Nutrition. Plants make their own food by a process called Photosynthesis. They take sunlight, water from roots, and carbon dioxide from air, and in the leaves, with the help of chlorophyll, they make glucose and release oxygen. The equation is:

Carbon dioxide + Water + Sunlight → Glucose + Oxygen.
6CO2 + 12H2O —(Chlorophyll / Sunlight)→ C6H12O6 (Glucose) + 6O2 + 6H2O
{{ render_part_footer(1, 'leaf', 'Make Plant Food: Photosynthesis Drag & Drop') }}
{{ render_smart_stage(1, 'eq1', '/static/images/saxaz_web/summary_sprites/anim_nutrition_fuel.webp?v=541') }}

In humans and animals, it is called Heterotrophic Nutrition, because we cannot make our own food, we depend on plants and animals. In humans, digestion starts in the mouth, where teeth chew food and saliva breaks down starch. Then food goes to the stomach, where acid and enzymes break down proteins. Then it goes to the small intestine, where most digestion and absorption happens. The digested food goes into the blood, and undigested waste goes to the large intestine and is removed. So nutrition gives us the fuel to live.

Mouth: The human digestive system starts from the mouth. The mouth is the first major part.

Respiration- Breathing to get Energy

Respiration is not just breathing. Breathing is only taking in oxygen and giving out carbon dioxide. Real respiration happens inside every cell. The food we eat, mainly glucose, is broken down with the help of oxygen to release energy. This energy is stored in a molecule called ATP, which is called the energy currency of the cell. The equation is:

Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy (ATP).
{{ render_part_footer(3, 'breathe', 'Breathing & ATP Energy: Drag & Match') }}
{{ render_smart_stage(3, 'eq3', '/static/images/saxaz_web/summary_sprites/anim_lungs_breathe.webp?v=541') }}

In humans, air enters through the nose, goes to the trachea, then to the lungs, where oxygen goes into the blood and carbon dioxide comes out. The blood carries oxygen to all cells. If we do heavy exercise, sometimes we get less oxygen, then anaerobic respiration happens in muscles and produces lactic acid, which is why we feel muscle pain. Without respiration, we will not get energy, even if we eat a lot of food.

How is Breathing Controlled?

Breathing is controlled by the brain. There are special centres in the brain that control breathing. There are two centres in the Pons and two centres in the Medulla that control breathing.

3. Transportation - Delivery System Of The Body

Transportation means carrying useful substances to every part of the body and taking waste away. In humans, the main organ is the Heart. It is like a pump with 4 chambers - right atrium, right ventricle, left atrium, left ventricle. The heart pumps blood through three types of blood vessels. Arteries carry oxygen-rich blood from heart to body, Veins carry carbon dioxide-rich blood from body to heart, and Capillaries are very thin vessels where exchange of oxygen, food, and waste happens between blood and cells.

{{ render_part_footer(6, 'heart', 'Build & Pump the Human Heart') }}
{{ render_smart_stage(6, 'eq6', '/static/images/saxaz_web/syllabus/nutshell_p2_img2.webp?v=541') }}

Blood has RBC which carries oxygen, WBC which fights disease, Platelets which help in clotting, and Plasma which carries food, hormones, and waste.

In plants, transportation happens through two tissues. Xylem carries water and minerals from roots to leaves, upwards. Phloem carries food made in leaves to all other parts of the plant, both upwards and downwards. This is called translocation.

Without transportation, food and oxygen will stay in one place and other parts will die.

4. Excretion - Cleaning The Body

Excretion means removing harmful waste products from the body. When our body uses food and energy, it produces waste like urea, extra water, salts, and carbon dioxide. If these stay inside, they will poison us. In humans, the main excretory organs are the Kidneys. We have two kidneys. Blood comes to the kidney, and inside the kidney there are small filtering units called Nephrons. Each kidney has about 1 million nephrons. Nephrons filter the blood, remove urea and extra water, and form urine.

{{ render_part_footer(9, 'kidney', 'Kidney Filter Sort: Drag Waste Out') }}
{{ render_smart_stage(9, 'eq9', '/static/images/saxaz_web/syllabus/nutshell_p3_img0.webp?v=541') }}

Urine goes from kidney through ureter to urinary bladder, and then out through urethra. Other ways of excretion are lungs removing carbon dioxide, skin removing sweat, and liver removing extra bile pigments. In plants, there is no special excretory system. Plants remove waste through transpiration from stomata, by storing waste in old leaves which fall off, and as resins and gums in bark. So excretion keeps our body clean from inside.

We are the Filters of the Body

Kidneys are called the filters of the body because they clean our blood. They remove waste, extra salt, and dirty things from the blood and send them out as urine, while keeping the good and useful things inside the body.

Conclusion: So, Nutrition gives us food, Respiration gives us energy from that food, Transportation delivers food and oxygen to every cell, and Excretion removes the waste. All four work together like a team. If one team member fails, the whole team fails. That is why they are called Life Processes.
